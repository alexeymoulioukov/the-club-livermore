    Amenities

    Everything is a short stroll from the clubhouse — courts, water, fitness, and a table waiting for you afterwards.

    Tennis & Pickleball

    Championship Courts

    Lighted hard courts, weekly clinics, junior programs, ladders and social mixers for every level — from first serve to club championship.

    Pool & Cabana

    The Pool Deck

    A resort-style pool with lap lanes, a shallow family area, shaded cabanas and poolside food and drink service all summer long.

    Dining & Social

    Clubhouse Dining

    Seasonal menus, a terrace overlooking the courts, wine-country evenings, and private rooms for birthdays, weddings and gatherings.

    🏋️ Fitness Studio

    Early-morning strength and yoga classes, personal training, and a bright studio that looks out over the grounds.

    👧 Junior Programs

    After-school tennis, swim lessons, summer camps and family nights that make the club a second home for kids.

    🎾 Pro Shop & Lessons

    Stringing, fittings and private lessons with our resident pros — book a session and sharpen the part of your game you love most.

    Golf

    Golf days woven into valley life — play, practise, and finish on the terrace.

    Golf at the Club

    Fairways beside the vines

    Members enjoy arranged tee times at neighbouring valley courses, a travelling golf group, monthly scrambles, and clinics for new golfers. It is golf the Livermore way — competitive when you want it, social always.

    • Monthly member scrambles & twilight nines
    • Lessons and junior golf introductions
    • Reciprocal days with partner clubs
